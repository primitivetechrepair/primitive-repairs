Back Glass Repair FAQs

Common questions about back glass repair in Miami.

Do you repair cracked iPhone back glass?

Yes. We provide back glass repair for supported iPhone models with cracked, shattered, missing, or damaged rear glass. The repair method depends on the exact model and whether the frame, cameras, wireless charging system, or housing is also damaged.

Do you repair Samsung rear glass?

Yes. We repair supported Samsung Galaxy rear panels, camera lens glass, housing damage, and related wireless charging concerns, depending on the exact model and part availability.

Can only the back glass be replaced?

Sometimes. A glass-only repair may be appropriate when the frame and housing remain structurally sound. Bent frames, crushed corners, damaged camera surrounds, or extensive housing damage may require a different repair method.

Do you repair cracked camera lens glass?

Yes. We review cracked or missing camera lens covers and test the camera for blur, glare, focus problems, contamination, and internal damage before confirming the repair.

Can broken back glass affect wireless charging?

Rear impact may affect the wireless charging coil, alignment, MagSafe components where applicable, or nearby internal parts. Wireless charging is tested when the reported symptoms indicate possible internal damage.

What should I include in the repair request?

Include the exact phone model, whether the glass is cracked, shattered, missing, or loose, whether the camera area is damaged, and whether wireless charging, liquid exposure, or other symptoms are present.

Is pricing confirmed before repair work begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the phone model, repair method, glass and housing condition, camera-area damage, required parts, and inspection findings. Pricing and available options are provided for approval before paid repair work begins.

Do back glass repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
