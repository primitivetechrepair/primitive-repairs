Compatibility-Led Planning

Every part must work with the system around it.

A fast component is not automatically the right component. Fit, power, cooling, firmware, memory support, and workload matter. Build and upgrade planning evaluates the full platform before installation.

01

Platform & Firmware

CPU socket, chipset, motherboard revision, BIOS support, memory generation, PCIe capability, storage interfaces, and required firmware are reviewed together.

02

Power & Physical Fit

Power-supply capacity, connector standards, GPU dimensions, radiator clearance, CPU cooler height, expansion slots, and case airflow determine whether parts fit safely.

03

Thermals & Acoustics

Heat output, fan placement, pressure balance, cooler orientation, cable obstruction, room conditions, and acceptable noise levels shape the cooling plan.

04

Real-World Gaming Goals

Target games, resolution, refresh rate, streaming, content work, storage needs, connectivity, and future upgrades guide where the budget creates meaningful value.

Before Your Appointment

Prepare the details that make diagnosis and planning faster.

Accurate specifications and a clear symptom history help separate a failed component from compatibility, driver, firmware, temperature, or operating-system problems.

📋

Record Current Specifications

Include component models, Windows version, monitor resolution, network connection, and any part recently installed or removed. Photos of labels or the current build can help when model details are uncertain.

💾

Back Up Important Files

When the PC is stable enough to use safely, maintain a current backup of important files, credentials, recovery keys, licenses, and configuration details before hardware or software service.

🔒

Bring Required Access

Software work may require the appropriate Windows, Microsoft, device, or game-platform credentials. Do not include passwords in the initial form; access needs are discussed securely if required.

Gaming PC FAQs

Common questions about gaming PC service in Miami.

What gaming PC problems do you diagnose?

We review supported gaming PCs with no-power, no-display, startup, crashing, overheating, fan-noise, storage, memory, graphics, driver, network, and performance concerns. The repair depends on inspection findings and component compatibility.

Can you build a custom gaming PC?

Yes. Custom build support can include compatibility planning, component installation, cable management, cooling setup, BIOS configuration, Windows installation, driver setup, and stability testing. The final scope depends on the selected components.

Can you install a new GPU, CPU, RAM, or SSD?

Yes, on compatible systems. We review the motherboard, case clearance, power supply, cooling, firmware, connectors, and operating-system requirements before confirming an installation.

Do you install Windows and gaming drivers?

Software support can include Windows installation or reset, chipset and graphics drivers, device drivers, firmware review, updates, startup cleanup, and supported gaming-application configuration.

Can you help with overheating or loud fans?

Yes. Cooling service may include inspection of airflow, fans, heatsinks, liquid-cooling components, dust buildup, thermal material, fan curves, cable placement, and temperature behavior.

Should I buy parts before submitting a request?

It is usually better to submit the current specifications and goal first. Compatibility can depend on the motherboard, BIOS, power supply, case clearance, cooling, connectors, and operating system.

Which gaming PC brands and custom systems do you support?

We review supported Alienware and Dell Gaming, ASUS ROG and TUF, Lenovo Legion, HP OMEN and Victus, MSI, Acer Predator and Nitro, boutique, prebuilt, and custom-built gaming PCs. Service depends on the exact model, construction, condition, component compatibility, and part availability.

Will my files be erased during software service?

Some operating-system work can affect installed programs, settings, or files. Back up important data when the computer is stable enough to do so. Any required reset or reinstall is discussed before that work begins.

Is pricing confirmed before gaming PC work begins?

Yes. Pricing depends on the system, requested work, required components, compatibility, condition, and diagnostic findings. The scope and price are reviewed for approval before paid work begins.
