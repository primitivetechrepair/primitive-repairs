Phone Repair FAQs

Common questions about phone repair in Miami.

Which phone brands do you repair?

We review supported iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Motorola, OnePlus, Nokia, ASUS, Sony, LG, TCL, and other smartphone models. Availability depends on the exact model, required repair, condition, and parts availability.

Do you replace cracked phone screens?

Yes, on supported models. We review the LCD or OLED panel, touch function, frame condition, fingerprint or face-authentication components, prior repair work, and whether the phone shows signs of power.

Do you replace phone batteries?

Yes, on supported models. Battery replacement may be appropriate for rapid drain, short runtime, swelling, percentage instability, excessive heat, shutdowns, or batteries that no longer hold a charge.

Does a phone that will not charge always need a new port?

No. The cable, adapter, debris, Lightning or USB-C connector, battery, internal charging assembly, wireless-charging system, liquid damage, software, or board-level circuitry may produce similar symptoms.

Can you repair phone cameras, speakers, and microphones?

Yes, on supported models. We review camera modules, camera glass, speakers, earpieces, microphones, buttons, vibration, sensors, internal connectors, and related board-level faults.

Can liquid-damaged phones be repaired?

We review supported liquid-damaged phones for corrosion, charging faults, screen problems, camera condensation, speaker or microphone issues, boot loops, and board-level damage. Repairability depends on the extent and location of damage.

Can a black screen mean the phone still has power?

Yes. If the phone rings, vibrates, charges, connects to a computer, produces sounds, or otherwise starts normally, the problem may involve the display rather than complete no power.

Is pricing confirmed before phone repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact model, failed component, required parts, physical or liquid damage, prior repair work, foldable-device complexity, board-level work, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do phone repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
