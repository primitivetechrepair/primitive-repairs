Most console HDMI ports are soldered directly to the motherboard. A clean connector replacement already requires controlled soldering. Before Requesting Repair

Safe checks for no-signal and HDMI connection problems.

Basic cable and input testing can help rule out an accessory or television issue. Do not force a connector that no longer fits, continue twisting a loose cable, or repeatedly insert and remove a visibly damaged plug. Additional movement can worsen pin, pad, trace, or motherboard damage.

📺

Confirm the Correct Television Input

Verify that the display is set to the correct HDMI input and that another known-working device can produce an image through that input when possible.

TV Input Display Check
🔌

Try a Known-Working HDMI Cable

A damaged or unsupported cable can cause intermittent video, no signal, flickering, image artifacts, or resolution problems that resemble console damage.

Cable Test Known Working
⛔

Do Not Force the Connector

Stop if the HDMI cable will not insert normally or if the port moves inside the enclosure. Forcing it can bend pins, separate solder joints, or tear motherboard pads.

No Force Protect the Board

HDMI Port Repair FAQs

Common questions about HDMI port repair in Miami.

Which consoles do you repair HDMI ports on?

We review HDMI repair requests for supported PS5, PS4, Xbox Series, Xbox One, and other eligible devices. Availability depends on the exact model, board condition, connector availability, and repair requirements.

Does no signal always mean the HDMI port is broken?

No. The HDMI cable, television input, display settings, connector, motherboard pads, traces, filters, retimer or encoder circuit, power system, storage, graphics hardware, or another board fault may produce similar symptoms.

What are the signs of physical HDMI-port damage?

Common signs include bent or missing pins, a loose connector, a port that moves inside the enclosure, a cable that will not seat correctly, visible impact damage, and video that changes when the cable is moved.

Is console HDMI repair considered microsoldering?

Yes. Console HDMI connectors are generally soldered directly to the motherboard. Replacement requires controlled soldering, and additional microsoldering may be required for lifted pads, broken traces, missing components, or video-circuit faults.

Can an HDMI port be repaired after the pads are torn?

Sometimes. Repairability depends on which pads or traces are missing, the amount of board damage, whether mounting points remain secure, previous repair attempts, and the condition of nearby components.

Do you repair PS5 HDMI ports?

Yes, on supported PlayStation 5 consoles. We review physical connector damage, bent or missing pins, no-signal symptoms, black screens, damaged pads, broken traces, and related board-level video faults.

Do you repair Xbox HDMI ports?

Yes, on supported Xbox consoles. We review loose or damaged connectors, no video, intermittent display, black screens, bent pins, motherboard damage, and related HDMI-circuit faults.

Is pricing confirmed before HDMI repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact console, connector condition, pad and trace damage, nearby component damage, previous repair work, required parts, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do HDMI repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
