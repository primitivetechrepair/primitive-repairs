Do you repair Galaxy Z Fold and Z Flip screens?

We review supported Fold and Flip devices for inner-screen, cover-screen, frame, hinge, battery, USB-C, and related symptoms. Availability depends on the exact model, damage, required assembly, and parts availability.

Yes, on supported models. Battery replacement may be appropriate for rapid drain, short runtime, swelling, percentage instability, excessive heat, shutdowns, or batteries that no longer hold a charge.

No. The cable, adapter, debris, USB-C connector, battery, internal charging assembly, wireless-charging system, liquid damage, software, or board-level circuitry may produce similar symptoms.

Can you repair Samsung cameras, speakers, and microphones?

Yes, on supported models. We review camera modules, camera glass, speakers, earpieces, microphones, buttons, vibration, S Pen systems, sensors, connectors, and related board faults.

Can liquid-damaged Samsung phones be repaired?

We review supported liquid-damaged phones for corrosion, charging faults, screen problems, camera condensation, speaker or microphone issues, boot loops, and board-level damage. Repairability depends on the extent and location of the damage.

Is pricing confirmed before Samsung repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact model, failed component, required parts, physical or liquid damage, foldable-device complexity, prior repair work, board-level work, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided before paid work begins.

Do Samsung repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
