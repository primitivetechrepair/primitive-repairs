Tablet Repair FAQs

Common questions about tablet repair in Miami.

Which tablet brands do you repair?

We review supported Apple iPad, Samsung Galaxy Tab, Microsoft Surface, Amazon Fire, Lenovo, TCL, Nokia, ASUS, and other Android tablet models. Availability depends on the exact model, repair, condition, and parts availability.

Do you repair cracked tablet screens?

Yes, on supported models. We review the outer glass, LCD or OLED panel, touch layer, frame condition, stylus or biometric components, prior repair work, and whether the tablet shows signs of power.

Do you replace tablet batteries?

Yes, on supported models. Battery replacement may be appropriate for rapid drain, short runtime, swelling, percentage instability, excessive heat, shutdowns, or a tablet that works only while connected to power.

Does a tablet that will not charge always need a new port?

No. The cable, adapter, debris, Lightning or USB-C connector, battery, internal charging assembly, liquid damage, software, dock, or board-level circuitry may produce similar symptoms.

Can you repair tablet cameras, speakers, and microphones?

Yes, on supported models. We review camera modules, camera glass, speakers, microphones, buttons, keyboards, stylus systems, sensors, internal connectors, and related board faults.

Can liquid-damaged tablets be repaired?

We review supported liquid-damaged tablets for corrosion, charging faults, display and touch problems, camera condensation, audio failure, boot loops, and board damage. Repairability depends on the extent and location of damage.

What should I do if the tablet screen is lifting?

Stop charging when safe if battery swelling may be present. Do not press the display back into position. Separation may also involve failed adhesive, heat, frame damage, contamination, or prior repair work.

Is pricing confirmed before tablet repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact tablet, display or battery assembly, charging system, physical or liquid damage, stylus or keyboard systems, prior repair work, board-level work, required parts, and inspection findings.

Do tablet repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
