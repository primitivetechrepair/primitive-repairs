iPhone Repair FAQs

Common questions about iPhone repair in Miami.

Which iPhone models do you repair?

We review repair requests for supported standard, Plus, Pro, Pro Max, SE, and older iPhone models. Availability depends on the exact model, device condition, required repair, and replacement-part availability.

Do you replace cracked iPhone screens?

Yes, on supported models. We review the OLED or LCD panel, touch function, frame condition, proximity and front-sensor behavior, prior repair work, and whether the phone is showing signs of power before confirming replacement.

Do you replace iPhone batteries?

Yes, on supported models. Battery replacement may be appropriate for rapid drain, short runtime, swelling, percentage instability, unexpected shutdowns, or batteries that no longer hold a charge.

Does an iPhone that will not charge always need a new port?

No. The cable, adapter, compacted debris, Lightning or USB-C connector, battery, wireless-charging system, internal flex, liquid damage, software, or board-level charging circuitry may produce similar symptoms.

Can Face ID problems be repaired?

Repairability depends on the exact failed component, model, impact or liquid damage, prior repairs, and the condition of the original security-sensitive sensor system. Some Face ID problems require advanced diagnosis and may not be recoverable through ordinary component replacement.

Do you repair iPhone back glass and camera damage?

Yes, on supported models. We review back-glass damage, camera-surround damage, rear housing condition, wireless charging, camera glass, camera modules, and frame alignment before confirming repair.

Can liquid-damaged iPhones be repaired?

We review supported liquid-damaged iPhones for corrosion, charging faults, screen problems, camera condensation, speaker or microphone issues, boot loops, Face ID symptoms, and board-level damage. Repair options depend on the extent and location of the damage.

Is pricing confirmed before iPhone repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact iPhone model, failed component, required parts, physical or liquid damage, prior repair work, specialized testing, board-level work, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do iPhone repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
