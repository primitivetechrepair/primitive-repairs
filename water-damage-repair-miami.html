Liquid Type and Immediate Actions

Why the liquid type and first response matter.

Different liquids leave different residues and corrosion risks. Powering on, charging, heating, shaking, or repeatedly testing a wet device can move liquid and create additional electrical damage.

💧

Clean Water and Rain

Fresh water may still carry minerals and contamination into connectors, microphones, speakers, cameras, batteries, shields, and motherboard areas.

Fresh Water Rain
🌊

Salt Water and Pool Water

Salt, chlorine, and dissolved minerals can be highly conductive and corrosive, accelerating damage to contacts, connectors, solder joints, pads, traces, and components.

Salt Water Chlorine
🥤

Coffee, Soda and Sugary Drinks

Sugars, acids, milk, flavoring, and sticky residue may remain after drying and interfere with buttons, keyboards, connectors, speakers, microphones, and board components.

Beverages Sticky Residue
🧪

Cleaning Products and Chemicals

Alcohol mixtures, soaps, detergents, disinfectants, oils, cosmetics, and other chemicals may attack coatings, adhesives, plastics, contacts, and electronic components.

Chemicals Residue

Condensation and Humidity

Water Damage Repair FAQs

Common questions about liquid-damage repair in Miami.

Which water-damaged devices do you repair?

We review supported phones, tablets, computers, game consoles, handheld systems, controllers, accessories, and other electronics. Availability depends on the device, liquid, corrosion, required parts, and repairability.

Should I charge a device after it gets wet?

Avoid charging or repeated startup attempts until the device has been inspected. Applying power while moisture or conductive residue remains may create additional short circuits and corrosion damage.

Does placing a wet device in rice help?

Rice does not remove corrosion, salts, sugars, chemicals, or residue trapped beneath shields, connectors, chips, batteries, displays, and other internal assemblies.

Can a device work normally and fail later?

Yes. Corrosion may continue after the outside appears dry. Delayed charging, screen, camera, audio, battery, wireless, sensor, storage, and no-power failures are possible.

Can liquid damage cause charging and no-power problems?

Yes. Liquid may damage the charging port, battery, charging assembly, connectors, protection components, charging controller, power-management system, and motherboard rails.

Can data be recovered from a liquid-damaged device?

Sometimes. Data access depends on the storage, motherboard, processor, encryption, power systems, corrosion, and whether enough stable operation can be restored to unlock and back up the device.

Can every liquid-damaged device be repaired?

No. Severe corrosion, damaged board layers, failed storage, burned circuits, missing components, unavailable parts, battery damage, or extensive previous repair work may make a stable repair impractical.

Is pricing confirmed before water damage repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the device, liquid type, corrosion, affected systems, required cleaning, replacement parts, board-level work, data priority, previous repair work, and inspection findings.

Do water damage repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
