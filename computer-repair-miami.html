Damage and Safety

What to do after liquid exposure, swelling, or overheating.

Stop using or charging the computer when safe if the battery is visibly swollen, the enclosure is separating, liquid was spilled inside, the charging connector is damaged, or the computer becomes unusually hot, smokes, or produces an odor. Do not force a lifted case closed or continue powering a liquid-exposed computer solely to test whether it still works.

💧

Liquid Exposure

Disconnect power when safe, stop repeated startup attempts, and include the liquid type, affected area, and timing in the repair request.

Liquid Damage Corrosion Risk

Computer Repair FAQs

Common questions about computer repair in Miami.

Which computers do you repair?

We provide repair services for supported Windows laptops, desktop PCs, Macs, MacBooks, all-in-one computers, and other eligible computer models. Service depends on the exact model, device condition, repair requirements, and part availability.

Do you repair broken laptop screens?

Yes, on supported models. We review the display size, resolution, connector, touch capability, mounting points, lid condition, hinges, and whether the problem involves the screen panel or another display component.

Why does my computer turn on but show no image?

The problem may involve the display panel, backlight, display cable, graphics system, memory, motherboard, power system, or operating-system startup. Testing helps separate no-display symptoms from complete no-power failure.

Can overheating cause shutdowns or slow performance?

Yes. Restricted airflow, failed fans, dust buildup, degraded thermal material, heavy workloads, or internal hardware problems may cause thermal throttling, instability, or shutdowns.

Does a laptop that will not charge always need a new port?

No. The power adapter, cable, charging port, battery, internal connector, charging circuit, software, or motherboard may produce similar symptoms. Inspection helps identify the correct repair.

Do you repair liquid-damaged computers?

We review supported liquid-damaged computers for corrosion, residue, connector damage, keyboard or trackpad failure, charging problems, no-power symptoms, and board-level damage. Repair options depend on the extent of the damage.

Should I back up my computer before repair?

Yes, when the computer is operating and it is safe to do so. Customers are responsible for maintaining current backups of important files, accounts, passwords, and software before service.

Is pricing confirmed before computer repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact model, failed component, required parts, physical or liquid damage, software requirements, board-level work, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do computer repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
