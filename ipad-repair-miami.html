iPad Repair FAQs

Common questions about mobile iPad repair in Miami.

Which iPad models do you repair?

We provide repair support for standard iPad, iPad Air, iPad mini, and iPad Pro models. Available repairs depend on the exact generation, device condition, and part availability.

Do you repair cracked iPad screens?

Yes. We review cracked glass, failed touch, display lines, LCD damage, black spots, flickering, blank screens, and other screen symptoms. The correct repair depends on whether the model uses separate glass and LCD components or a laminated display assembly.

Do you replace iPad batteries?

Yes. We help with fast battery drain, unexpected shutdowns, overheating, poor battery life, charging concerns, swelling, and screens that are lifting because of internal battery pressure.

Do you repair iPad charging ports?

Yes. We review Lightning and USB-C charging problems, loose cable connections, damaged pins, charging interruptions, debris, and iPads that no longer charge correctly.

Do you repair water-damaged iPads?

Yes. We review liquid-exposed iPads for moisture, corrosion, charging failures, no-power symptoms, screen problems, camera issues, speaker failures, and related damage.

Do you provide mobile iPad repair service?

Yes. Primitive Tech Repairs serves the Miami area through available mobile, meet-up, pickup, onsite, and mail-in service options. Available options are confirmed after the repair request is reviewed.

Is pricing confirmed before repair work begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact iPad model, repair type, device condition, required parts, and inspection findings. Available options and pricing are provided for approval before paid repair work begins.

Do iPad repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
