Before Requesting Repair

Safe checks for common console problems.

Basic cable, input, and power testing may help rule out an accessory problem. Do not force damaged connectors, continue using a console that is severely overheating, or repeatedly power a liquid-exposed system solely to test whether it still works.

📺

Check the Television Input

Confirm the correct HDMI input and test a known-working cable and display when available. This helps separate an accessory problem from console damage.

Input Cable Test
⛔

Do Not Force Damaged Ports

Stop if a connector will not insert normally. Twisting, forcing, or repeatedly reconnecting a damaged HDMI or USB-C port can worsen pin, pad, or board damage.

No Force Protect Connector
🌡️

Stop After Severe Overheating

Shut the console down when safe if it repeatedly displays heat warnings, turns off from temperature, produces a burning odor, or has a fan that is grinding or not operating.

Heat Warning Cooling Failure

Does no signal always mean the HDMI port is broken?

No. The HDMI cable, television input, video settings, connector, damaged pads or traces, filters, retimer or encoder circuitry, graphics hardware, power, storage, or another board fault may produce similar symptoms.

Can overheating cause a console to shut down?

Yes. Restricted airflow, dust buildup, failed fans, cooling problems, power faults, or internal hardware issues may cause heat warnings, reduced performance, instability, and shutdowns.

Do you repair Nintendo Switch USB-C ports?

Yes, on supported models. Charging in only one cable direction, loose connections, no charging, dock failures, and visible connector damage may involve the USB-C port or nearby charging circuitry.

Can liquid-damaged consoles be repaired?

We review supported liquid-damaged consoles for corrosion, connector damage, no-power symptoms, charging faults, display problems, and board-level damage. Repair options depend on the extent and location of the damage.

Is pricing confirmed before console repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact console model, failed component, required parts, physical or liquid damage, soldering requirements, previous repair work, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do game-console repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
