Before Requesting Repair

Safe checks for a device that will not charge.

Basic accessory testing may help identify a damaged cable or adapter. Do not insert metal objects, liquids, adhesives, or excessive force into the charging port. Stop using the device if the connector, cable, adapter, or battery becomes unusually hot, damaged, swollen, smoking, or produces an odor.

🔌

Try a Known-Working Accessory

Test a compatible cable and power adapter that are known to work correctly. A damaged accessory can produce the same symptoms as a damaged charging port.

Cable Test Adapter Test
⛔

Do Not Force the Connector

Stop if the cable will not insert normally. Forcing or twisting it can damage internal contacts, break the connector, or compact debris deeper into the port.

No Force Protect Contacts
⚠️

Watch for Heat or Battery Swelling

Disconnect the charger when safe if the device becomes unusually hot, the screen or back panel begins lifting, or the battery appears swollen.

Heat Battery Safety

Charging Port Repair FAQs

Common questions about charging port repair in Miami.

Which charging ports do you repair?

We repair supported Lightning and USB-C charging ports on iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, iPad, tablets, and other eligible devices. Service depends on the model, connector construction, device condition, and part availability.

Why does my cable only work at an angle?

Angle-dependent charging may result from compacted debris, worn internal contacts, a loose port, a damaged cable, bent components, broken solder joints, or movement in the charging assembly.

Can lint or debris prevent charging?

Yes. Compacted debris can prevent the connector from seating completely and may cause a loose or intermittent connection. Inspection determines whether careful cleaning is sufficient or whether the connector is physically damaged.

Does a device that will not charge always need a new port?

No. The cable, adapter, battery, charging port, internal connector, liquid damage, software, or board-level charging circuitry can create similar symptoms. The charging path should be reviewed before replacing the port.

Can liquid exposure damage the charging port?

Yes. Moisture and residue may affect the port contacts, connectors, charging assembly, or board-level circuitry. Corrosion can continue developing after the visible liquid has dried.

Can a charging port also affect data transfer?

Yes. Depending on the device and connector design, damaged contacts or circuitry may affect charging, computer connection, accessory recognition, audio, display output, or data transfer.

Is pricing confirmed before charging-port repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact model, connector condition, required assembly, liquid or impact damage, soldering requirements, replacement parts, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do charging-port repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
