Screen Repair FAQs

Common questions about screen repair in Miami.

Which devices do you repair screens on?

We review supported iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Motorola, OnePlus, other smartphones, iPads, and tablets. Availability depends on the exact model, display type, damage, condition, and parts availability.

Do you replace both LCD and OLED screens?

Yes, on supported models. LCD, OLED, AMOLED, and foldable displays use different construction, connectors, frames, calibration requirements, and replacement assemblies.

Can the glass be cracked while the display still works?

Yes. The outer glass may be damaged while the image and touch still function. Continued use can allow cracks to spread, expose sharp edges, admit moisture, or place further stress on the display.

Does a black screen always mean the screen is broken?

No. A black screen may involve the display, connector, backlight, battery, charging system, incomplete startup, liquid damage, or motherboard. Sound, vibration, charging, and computer connection help distinguish these conditions.

Can ghost touch and dead touch zones be repaired?

Often, on supported models. These symptoms may involve the touch layer, display assembly, connector, frame pressure, moisture, charging interference, prior repair work, or a board-level fault.

Do you repair foldable phone screens?

We review supported foldable devices for inner-screen, cover-screen, hinge, frame, battery, and flex-cable symptoms. Availability depends on the exact model, failed assembly, physical condition, and parts availability.

What if the screen is lifting away from the frame?

Stop charging when safe if battery swelling may be present. Do not press the display back into place. Separation may also involve failed adhesive, frame damage, heat, contamination, or prior repair work.

Is pricing confirmed before screen repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact device, LCD or OLED assembly, frame condition, biometric components, foldable construction, physical or liquid damage, prior repair work, required parts, and inspection findings.

Do screen repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
