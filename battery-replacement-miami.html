Battery Safety

What to do when a battery is swollen or overheating.

A visibly swollen, damaged, leaking, smoking, or unusually hot battery should be treated as a safety concern. Stop charging or using the device when it is safe to do so, avoid pressing the screen or back panel into place, and keep the device away from heat, flammable materials, and puncture risks.

⛔

Do Not Apply Pressure

Do not force a lifted screen or separated back panel closed. Pressure can damage the display, enclosure, battery, or internal components.

No Pressure Handle Carefully
🔌

Stop Charging

Disconnect the device from power if the battery is swollen, smoking, leaking, or becoming unusually hot during charging.

Unplug Power Safety
🔥

Avoid Heat and Puncture

Do not puncture, bend, crush, heat, freeze, or attempt to remove a swollen battery without the correct tools and handling procedures.

No Puncture No Heat

Battery Replacement FAQs

Common questions about battery replacement in Miami.

Which device batteries do you replace?

We provide battery replacement for supported iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, iPad, tablet, and other device models. Available service depends on the exact model, device condition, and part availability.

How do I know whether the battery is failing?

Common symptoms include rapid drain, poor runtime, unexpected shutdowns, overheating, swelling, charging concerns, warning messages, and reduced performance. Testing may be needed because charging or power-system faults can produce similar symptoms.

Is a swollen battery dangerous?

A swollen battery is a safety concern because it can place pressure on the screen, enclosure, and internal components. Stop charging or using the device when safe, avoid pressing the device closed, and arrange inspection promptly.

Will a new battery fix charging problems?

Not always. Charging problems may come from the battery, charging port, cable, adapter, connector, charging circuit, or software. The reported symptoms and device condition are reviewed before recommending a battery replacement.

Can a weak battery cause unexpected shutdowns?

Yes. A worn or failing battery may shut down under load or at higher displayed percentages. Other power, software, or board-level problems can produce similar behavior and may require additional diagnosis.

Is pricing confirmed before replacement begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact device model, replacement battery, device condition, required parts, and inspection findings. Pricing and service options are provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do battery replacements include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement battery installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
