Liquid, Power and Safety

What to do after liquid exposure, swelling, or severe heat.

Stop using or charging the phone when safe if liquid entered the device, the battery is swollen, the screen or rear cover is lifting, the USB-C port is visibly damaged, or the phone becomes unusually hot, smokes, or produces an odor.

💧

Liquid Exposure

Disconnect charging accessories when safe, stop repeated startup attempts, and include the liquid type, affected area, and timing in the repair request.

Liquid Damage Corrosion Risk
⚠️

Swollen Battery

Stop charging when safe and do not press a lifted screen or rear cover back into position. Battery expansion can damage the display, frame, and nearby internal components.

Battery Safety No Pressure

Unusual Heat or Odor

Overheating Power Safety

Motorola Repair FAQs

Common questions about Motorola repair in Miami.

Which Motorola phones do you repair?

We review repair requests for supported Moto G, Motorola Edge, Motorola Razr, Motorola One, ThinkPhone, and older Motorola models. Availability depends on the exact model, device condition, required repair, and parts availability.

Do you replace cracked Motorola screens?

Yes, on supported models. We review the LCD or OLED panel, touch function, frame condition, fingerprint-sensor location, prior repair work, and whether the phone is showing signs of power before confirming replacement.

Do you repair Motorola Razr foldable screens?

Supported Razr requests are reviewed case by case. Inner displays, external screens, hinges, frames, batteries, and internal flexible components have different repair requirements and part availability.

Do you replace Motorola batteries?

Yes, on supported models. Battery replacement may be appropriate for rapid drain, short runtime, swelling, percentage instability, excessive heat, unexpected shutdowns, or batteries that no longer hold a charge.

Does a Motorola phone that will not charge always need a USB-C port?

No. The cable, adapter, compacted debris, USB-C connector, battery, internal flex, wireless-charging system, liquid damage, software, or board-level charging circuitry may produce similar symptoms.

Do you repair Motorola camera and audio problems?

Yes, on supported models. We review camera modules, protective camera glass, speakers, earpieces, microphones, buttons, vibration, sensors, internal connectors, and related board-level faults.

Can liquid-damaged Motorola phones be repaired?

We review supported liquid-damaged devices for corrosion, charging faults, screen problems, camera condensation, speaker or microphone issues, boot loops, and board-level damage. Repair options depend on the extent and location of the damage.

Is pricing confirmed before Motorola repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact model, failed component, required parts, physical or liquid damage, prior repair work, foldable-device complexity, board-level work, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do Motorola repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
