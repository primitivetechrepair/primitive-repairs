Pixel Safety

What to do after swelling, liquid exposure, or severe heat.

Stop using or charging the phone when safe if the battery is swollen, the screen or rear cover is lifting, liquid entered the device, the USB-C port is visibly damaged, or the phone becomes unusually hot, smokes, or produces an odor. Do not press separated panels back into position or repeatedly power a liquid-exposed phone solely to test whether it still works.

⚠️

Swollen Battery

Stop charging the phone when safe and avoid pressure on a lifted screen or rear cover. Battery expansion can damage the display, frame, wireless-charging assembly, and nearby internal components.

Battery Safety No Pressure
💧

Liquid Exposure

Disconnect charging accessories when safe, stop repeated startup attempts, and include the liquid type, affected area, and timing in the repair request.

Liquid Damage Corrosion Risk
🌡️

Unusual Heat

Stop charging when safe if the phone becomes extremely hot, repeatedly shuts down, heats near the USB-C connector, or produces an odor. Heat may involve the battery, connector, cable, adapter, or charging circuit.

Overheating Charging Safety

Google Pixel Repair FAQs

Common questions about Google Pixel repair in Miami.

Which Google Pixel models do you repair?

We review repair requests for supported standard Pixel, Pixel Pro, Pixel XL, Pixel A-series, Pixel Fold, and older Pixel models. Availability depends on the exact model, device condition, required repair, and replacement-part availability.

Do you replace cracked Google Pixel screens?

Yes, on supported models. We review the OLED panel, touch function, frame condition, fingerprint-related components, prior repair work, and whether the phone is producing signs of power before confirming screen replacement.

Will the fingerprint sensor work after screen repair?

Fingerprint-system requirements vary by Pixel model and replacement display. Some repairs may require model-specific testing, calibration, setup, or additional troubleshooting before biometric operation can be confirmed.

Do you replace Google Pixel batteries?

Yes, on supported devices. Battery replacement may be appropriate for rapid drain, short runtime, swelling, unexpected shutdowns, excessive heat, percentage instability, or a battery that no longer holds a charge.

Does a Pixel that will not charge always need a USB-C port?

No. The cable, adapter, compacted debris, USB-C connector, battery, internal flex, wireless-charging system, liquid damage, software, or board-level charging circuitry may produce similar symptoms.

Do you repair Pixel camera glass and camera modules?

Yes, on supported models. Cracked protective camera glass is different from a failed camera module. Focus problems, shaking, black screens, application errors, or distorted images may require camera-module or internal diagnosis.

Can liquid-damaged Google Pixel phones be repaired?

We review supported liquid-damaged Pixel devices for corrosion, charging faults, display problems, camera condensation, speaker or microphone issues, boot loops, and board-level damage. Repair options depend on the extent and location of the damage.

Is pricing confirmed before Pixel repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact Pixel model, failed component, required parts, physical or liquid damage, prior repair work, calibration requirements, board-level work, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do Google Pixel repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
