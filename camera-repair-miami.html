Camera Repair FAQs

Common questions about camera repair in Miami.

Which phone and tablet cameras do you repair?

We provide camera repair for supported iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, iPad, tablet, and other device models. Available service depends on the exact model, camera system, device condition, and part availability.

Can cracked camera lens glass be replaced?

Yes, on supported models. The exterior camera lens cover may be replaceable when the surrounding housing and internal camera remain in usable condition. The camera is tested for blur, glare, focus problems, contamination, and internal damage.

Does blurry video mean the camera needs replacement?

Not always. Blurry images may result from damaged lens glass, contamination, autofocus failure, stabilization problems, internal camera damage, software behavior, or impact-related misalignment. Inspection helps identify the correct repair.

Why does my phone camera shake or vibrate?

Camera shaking may involve the stabilization system, autofocus mechanism, internal camera module, impact damage, magnetic interference, or another hardware problem. The camera behavior and damage history should be reviewed before replacing parts.

Why does the camera application show a black screen?

A black screen may come from a failed camera module, damaged connector, software issue, application problem, liquid exposure, impact damage, or another internal fault. Testing helps determine whether the issue affects one camera or the complete camera system.

Can camera damage happen after the phone is dropped?

Yes. Impact can crack the lens cover, damage the internal camera, affect stabilization or autofocus, bend the camera ring, damage the housing, or loosen internal connections.

Is pricing confirmed before camera repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the device model, affected camera, lens glass condition, internal camera damage, surrounding housing, required parts, and inspection findings. Pricing and service options are provided for approval before paid repair work begins.

Do camera repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
