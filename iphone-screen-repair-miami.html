iPhone Screen Repair FAQs

Quick answers before you book.

Do you come to me for iPhone screen repair?

Yes. Mobile and onsite service is available for supported repairs in the Miami area. Meet-up, pickup, and mail-in options may also be available depending on the request.

Can you fix a broken iPhone screen near me?

Yes. We provide mobile phone repair for supported iPhone screen replacements throughout Miami, so many customers can have the repair completed at their location instead of visiting a repair shop. Current appointment availability is confirmed when you submit the request.

Can you repair an iPhone screen the same day?

Many supported screen replacements can be completed the same day when the correct part and appointment are available. Timing is confirmed after the exact model and damage are reviewed.

Does a cracked screen always need the complete display replaced?

Most mobile iPhone screen repairs use a complete display assembly. The correct repair depends on the model, panel condition, touch function, frame damage, and available parts.

Will Face ID still work after a screen replacement?

Existing Face ID and front-sensor hardware is preserved when it is undamaged and serviceable. A pre-existing sensor or biometric failure may require separate diagnosis.

Do you use OLED or LCD replacement screens?

The compatible display technology and available replacement options depend on the exact iPhone model. We confirm the screen option and final price before repair.

Will screen replacement erase my data?

A normal screen replacement does not require erasing the iPhone. A current backup is still recommended whenever possible, especially if the phone has severe impact, liquid, or startup damage.

Is the screen repair covered by a warranty?

Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty for the installed part and repair work. New physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, and unrelated failures are excluded.
