No Power Repair FAQs

Common questions about no-power repair in Miami.

Can you repair a device that will not turn on?

Many no-power faults can be repaired, but the correct repair depends on the device, charging system, battery, display, liquid or impact damage, previous repairs, board condition, required parts, and diagnostic findings.

Does a black screen always mean the device has no power?

No. If the device rings, vibrates, runs a fan, produces sound, connects to a charger or computer, or otherwise completes part of startup, the problem may involve the display or video-output system.

Does a device that will not charge always need a new port?

No. The charger, cable, power adapter, battery, debris, connector, internal charging assembly, power supply, liquid damage, software, or board-level circuitry may create similar symptoms.

Can a failed battery make a device appear completely dead?

Yes. A failed, disconnected, deeply discharged, swollen, or internally protected battery may prevent startup or cause the device to shut down immediately after external power is removed.

Can liquid damage cause complete no power?

Yes. Liquid exposure can damage charging connectors, batteries, power rails, storage, displays, power-management circuits, small components, and internal board layers.

Should I continue trying to charge the device?

Stop charging when safe if there was liquid exposure, swelling, extreme heat, smoke, an unusual odor, visible connector damage, or severe physical damage. Include those details in the repair request.

Can every no-power device be repaired?

No. Some devices have excessive corrosion, burned board layers, unavailable parts, severe impact damage, destroyed storage, previous-repair damage, or a repair cost and risk that make restoration impractical.

Is pricing confirmed before no-power repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact device, diagnostic requirements, failed component, battery or charging condition, liquid or physical damage, board-level work, required parts, and repairability. Pricing is provided before paid work begins.

Do no-power repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
