Meta Glasses FAQs

Common questions about Meta smart glasses repair in Miami.

Do you repair Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses?

We review repair and diagnostic requests for supported Ray-Ban Meta glasses. Available service depends on the exact model, symptoms, failed component, physical condition, internal access, and replacement-part availability.

Can you repair a Meta charging case?

Charging-case problems are reviewed for cable-input failure, damaged contacts, alignment problems, contamination, physical damage, inconsistent charging, and cases that no longer recognize the glasses.

Why do the glasses charge inconsistently?

Possible causes include contaminated contacts, improper seating, frame or case misalignment, a damaged case, glasses- side contact failure, battery problems, liquid exposure, or another internal power fault.

Can you fix Bluetooth or application pairing problems?

We can review pairing and setup symptoms. The cause may involve phone settings, application permissions, account connection, firmware, network access, battery state, physical controls, or glasses-side hardware.

Do you repair speaker, microphone, or camera problems?

We review supported devices with one-sided audio, distortion, microphone problems, capture failures, button issues, camera errors, or symptoms that began after impact or liquid exposure.

Can you repair broken frames or hinges?

Repairability depends on the location and severity of the damage, whether electronics or wiring are affected, the availability of structural parts, and whether the glasses can be safely aligned and reassembled.

Do you disable camera recording indicators?

No. We do not disable, conceal, bypass, or defeat recording indicators, privacy safeguards, security controls, or safety protections.

Is pricing confirmed before Meta glasses repair begins?

Yes. Final pricing depends on the exact model, failed component, physical or liquid damage, required parts, internal access, previous modification attempts, and inspection findings. Pricing is provided for approval before paid work begins.

Do Meta glasses repairs include a warranty?

Yes. Repairs performed by Primitive Tech Repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty covering the replacement parts installed and repair work performed. The warranty does not cover new physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, tampering, or unrelated device failures.
